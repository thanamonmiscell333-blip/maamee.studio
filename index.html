Mudmee ikat.
Made for dogs
for the first time.

Mudmee ikat has been woven the same way for hundreds of years — by hand, in Thailand, using a resist-dyeing technique passed down through generations. It has been worn to ceremonies, to weddings, to temples.

It has never been made for dogs. Until now.

"No two dogs wear the same pattern. That's not a flaw — that's the point."
